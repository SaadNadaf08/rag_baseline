# Expense Policy (Acme Corp)

All business expenses must be submitted in the Spenddesk app within 30 days of purchase. Submissions older than 30 days are rejected automatically unless a VP approves an exception.

Reimbursable expenses include: economy flights, standard rail, rental cars (compact class), hotels up to 180 USD per night in North America and Europe, and meals up to 55 USD per day. Alcohol cannot be reimbursed, even during client dinners.

Personal car mileage is reimbursed at 0.67 USD per mile. Taxis and rideshare are reimbursable only for trips where public transit is unavailable or unsafe after 9 PM.

Any single expense above 500 USD requires pre-approval from your manager BEFORE the purchase. Hardware purchases above 250 USD must go through IT procurement, not the expense system.

The company card may be used for travel and team events only. All other purchases must be paid personally and reimbursed.
