# Security Handbook (Acme Corp)

Every employee must enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on their company accounts during onboarding week. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and must never be reused across services. The password manager is 1Password; storing passwords in browsers, notes apps, or spreadsheets is a policy violation.

Company laptops are encrypted and automatically lock after 10 minutes of inactivity. Installing software requires approval through the IT self-service portal. Unauthorized VPN tools, crypto miners, and cracked software are strictly prohibited.

Suspected phishing emails must be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook, not deleted. Reporting a real phishing attempt earns a 50 USD gift card credit; clicking a phishing link requires a mandatory 30-minute refresher training.

When working from public spaces, employees must use the company VPN on any untrusted Wi-Fi network. Customer data may only be accessed from company-managed devices. Personal devices may never access production systems.

In case of a suspected data breach, notify the security team at security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate or remediate on your own.
