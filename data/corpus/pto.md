# Vacation and PTO Policy (Acme Corp, 2026)

All full-time employees receive 24 paid time off (PTO) days per year, accrued at 2 days per month. New hires accrue PTO from their first day but may only take it after 90 days of employment.

PTO requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for requests of 5 or more consecutive days, and 3 days in advance for shorter absences. Managers respond within 2 business days.

Unused PTO rolls over, up to a maximum of 8 days into the next calendar year. Anything beyond 8 days is forfeited on December 31. PTO is not paid out when an employee leaves the company.

In addition to PTO, Acme observes 11 public holidays. Sick leave is unlimited but requires a doctor's note after 3 consecutive sick days.

Employees celebrating a milestone anniversary (5, 10, 15 years) receive 1 extra floating holiday in that year, which does not expire until the end of the following year.
