# New Hire Onboarding Guide (Acme Corp)

Your first week: Day 1 is remote. You receive your laptop by courier, sign documents in DocuSign, and meet your onboarding buddy. Days 2-3 are orientation sessions covering benefits, security training, and team introductions. By Day 5 you complete your first small starter ticket.

Your onboarding buddy is your go-to for everyday questions for the first 60 days. Your manager holds a 30/60/90 plan meeting in your first week and reviews progress at 30, 60, and 90 days.

Equipment: your laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse ship to your home address. Peripherals are replaced free of charge within the first year; after that, replacement requests go through your manager.

All new hires complete mandatory training during the first month: Security Awareness (2 hours), Anti-Harassment (1 hour), and Product Fundamentals (3 hours). Completion is tracked automatically in the LMS.

The onboarding checklist lives in the HR portal and must be 100 percent complete by the end of week 2, or your access to some systems is temporarily restricted.
